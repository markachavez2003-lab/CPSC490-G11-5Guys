## Project Summary

Our project is a marathon tracking app that allows runners to share their progress with friends and family during a race. The app will use GPS to track a runner's location and display their progress along the route. Runners will also be able to share pictures and short updates through a webpage that spectators can access. We also plan to include race time predictions based on running pace and previous performance.

## Project Goals

1. **Live Runner Tracking:** Track and display a runner's location during a race.
2. **Posts and Shareable Web Page:** Allow runners to share pictures and updates that spectators can view.
3. **Race Time Prediction:** Estimate a runner's finish time using their pace and previous runs.
